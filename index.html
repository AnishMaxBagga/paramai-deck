The bottleneck

Quantitative Systems Pharmacology engines can predict efficacy and toxicity before a single patient is dosed — if they are fed correct parameters.

Each simulation needs hundreds of kinetic constants: receptor binding rates, hepatic extraction ratios, partition coefficients, intrinsic clearances. That data is trapped in millions of unstructured papers.

01 / Manual

PhDs extract by hand

Highly paid pharmacometricians spend months per program. Most teams skip it entirely and revert to empirical dosing — which is exactly why 97% of oncology trials fail.

02 / Naive AI

Generic NLP scrapes garbage

Published parameters frequently violate the laws of physiology. Feed contradictory inputs to a QSP model and the simulation silently collapses.

03 / Stakes

Dose-finding is guessing

FDA's Project Optimus now mandates mathematical dose optimization in oncology. Empirical maximum-tolerated-dose is no longer defensible.

The architecture

Not an LLM with extra steps. An orchestrated agent system.

A stateful supervisor with embedded pharmacology expertise dispatches specialized worker agents, integrates their outputs into a unified internal understanding, and projects that understanding into regulator-defensible models via an interpretability layer.

Supervisor agent

Hybrid state + control policy

Structured beliefs with citations + learned latent representation. Decides which workers to dispatch, integrates results, transitions state.

The defensibility

The moat is the supervisor's embedded pharmacology — not the LLM.

Extraction is commoditizing. Academic systems already hit ≥99% precision on narrow domains. The moat lives downstream: in physics-validated parameters with a regulator-acceptable provenance trail that no extractor alone can produce.

Why now

Regulation is forcing the buyer.

Four documents in a 36-month window define the category. Build to ICH M15 from day one; everything else follows from it.

Dec 2022

FDA Modernization Act 2.0

Removes the legal mandate for animal testing. Explicitly permits computational models.

Aug 2024

Project Optimus

Final guidance. Mandates randomized dose-ranging and mathematical optimization in oncology.

Sept 2025

Computer Software Assurance

FDA finalizes risk-based assurance framework. Favorable to modern SaaS engineering.

Jul 2026

ICH M15 effective

Harmonizes US / EU / Japan / China on MIDD evidence. One common framework.
