Pharmacokinetics and bioavailability of gefitinib in healthy volunteers

Mean plasma clearance of gefitinib was CL = 28 L/h with an apparent volume of distribution Vd = 1400 L. Reported hepatic extraction ratio EH = 0.81 (portal sampling, n=8).

Urinary excretion accounted for CLR = 1.4 L/h. Oral bioavailability was F = 0.59, absorption rate ka = 0.5 1/h. Plasma protein binding gave fu = 0.09.
