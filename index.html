The bottleneck

Drug development still looks more like guessing than science.

The math exists. The simulators exist. But the parameters that drive them are trapped in millions of unstructured papers — and most of those parameters, when extracted naively, violate the laws of physiology.

01

The data is buried

Each simulation needs hundreds of kinetic constants. Highly paid PhDs spend months extracting them by hand. Most teams skip it entirely.

02

Naive extraction breaks physiology

Published parameters frequently contradict the laws of mass balance and blood flow. Garbage in, silent collapse out — and nobody knows the simulation lied.

03

Regulators are catching up

FDA's Project Optimus mandates mathematical dose optimization. Empirical maximum-tolerated-dose is no longer defensible.

Our approach

A biological world model — not a literature search.

We don't just read papers. We solve the underlying physiology. Every value ParamAI emits has been tested against the laws that govern how drugs actually move through the human body — and ships with the citation chain to prove it.

i

Mechanistic validation

Every extracted parameter is checked against mass balance, regional blood flow, partition equilibria, and protein binding. Contradictions are surfaced — not silently averaged.

ii

Inverse derivation

When the literature value violates physics, we lock established constants and solve the equations backwards for the value the paper never reported.

iii

Simulation-ready output

Output drops directly into the simulators pharma already runs. No file conversion, no parameter re-mapping, no human curation step.

iv

Submission-grade provenance

Every value ships with citation, derivation chain, and assumption ledger — the audit trail FDA reviewers need under emerging MIDD guidance.

Why now

Regulation is forcing the buyer.

Three recent FDA actions have shifted the ground under drug development. Mathematical modeling has moved from "optional acceleration" to "expected evidence" — and the simulators pharma already runs are starving for the verified inputs.

2022

FDA Modernization Act 2.0

Removed the legal mandate for animal testing. Computational models are now an explicitly permitted alternative.

2024

Project Optimus

Finalized guidance requiring evidence-based dose optimization in oncology. Empirical maximum-tolerated-dose is no longer enough.

2026

ICH M15 takes effect

A unified US / EU / Japan / China framework for model-informed drug development evidence. One audit trail, four regulators.
